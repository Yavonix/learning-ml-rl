## Geometric mean

The geometric mean tells you what number raised to $n$ gives the same product of $\left\{ a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n \right\}$

The geometric mean of a dataset $\left\{ a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n \right\}$ is given by:

$$
\left (\prod_{i=1}^{n} a_i \right)^\frac{1}{n} = \sqrt[n]{a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n}
$$
